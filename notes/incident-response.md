# Incident response notes

A place to study public incident-response guidance and to outline what a personal after-action note should capture. This file is conceptual. Do not add intrusion procedures, malware analysis steps, exploit reproduction, or tool command sequences.

## What an after-action note should capture

Use this outline when writing up a tabletop, a public incident report, or an exercise you ran on systems you administer.

- When the event was noticed, and when it was declared handled
- Which systems, data, and people were in scope
- Which decisions were made, and who made them
- Which controls already existed (backups, logging, access removal) and whether they were usable
- What will change afterward: a patch window, a contact, a logging gap, a runbook title
- Links to the public advisories or status pages you relied on

## Evidence handling (principles only)

- Preserve original copies; do analysis on copies
- Record who collected an artifact and when
- Do not put secrets, customer data, or malware samples in this repository
